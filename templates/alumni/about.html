About Our Institution

Anwar Majmau Shariath & Dawa College, Vidyanagar, Nilambur

Our Legacy

History of Anwar Majmau College

Established in Nilambur, Malappuram, Kerala, Anwar Majmau Shariath & Dawa College has served as a beacon of Islamic learning, moral excellence, and academic distinction for decades.

Situated in the serene locale of Vidyanagar, the college integrates traditional Shariath scholarship with contemporary Dawa methodologies and modern sciences, equipping young leaders to serve society with integrity and wisdom.

"Empowering scholars with deep spiritual values, social responsibility, and intellectual competence."

Guiding Principles

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To nurture holistic Islamic scholars who possess profound knowledge of sacred texts, exemplary character, and the practical capabilities required to address contemporary challenges, promote social harmony, and serve humanity.

Our Vision

To be an internationally recognized center of Islamic higher education and scholarly excellence, producing visionary leaders who enlighten communities through authentic knowledge, wisdom, and compassionate service.
